# Second-Year Dynamics

An active-learning textbook for second-year classical mechanics, developed for
Queen’s University’s PHYS 206 course by **Sarah Sadavoy**, with support from
**Cora Sleegers** and **Lance Schonberg**.

This web edition follows the **Fall 2025** source. The original is titled
*Untitled Dynamics Textbook (A work in progress)*.

Start with the [introduction](front/introduction.md), or go directly to
[Calculus and Vectors](chapters/ch-01-calculus-and-vectors.md). The book proceeds
through Newtonian motion, oscillations, accelerating and rotating frames,
momentum, energy, central forces, orbits, and the Lagrange method.

The chapters retain the source’s learning objectives, quick questions, worked
examples, student commentary, applications, summaries, and practice problems.
The appendices provide [resources](appendices/app-a-resources.md),
[derivations](appendices/app-b-derivations-and-approximations.md), and
[solutions](appendices/app-c-solutions-to-problems.md).
